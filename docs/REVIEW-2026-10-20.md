Its webhook key parser accepts the configured field template and verification reads WEBHOOK_SECRET_FRESHDESK. The optional body/header/route contract is now documented.

- Zoho search no longer assumes English Closed/Resolved labels or an unnecessary sort field. Returned and reread canonical statusType must be resolved/closed.
