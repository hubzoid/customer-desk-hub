# Synthetic Meridian Distribution

SYNTHETIC SAMPLE COMPANY. Every name, address and number here is invented for the demo. Contacts use reserved `.example` domains.

- A wholesale distributor of packaging and warehouse supplies to small and medium businesses.
- Customer care works 08:00 to 17:00 UTC, Monday to Friday. Replies go out in English.
- Currency is USD. Dates and times are UTC.
- Teams:
  - Customer care (desk operators) answers customers and sends replies.
  - The delivery team changes delivery dates in ERPNext.
  - The complaints team owns complaint tickets and escalation plans.
  - The exception team decides refunds and goodwill outside policy.
  - Binding admins match customer email addresses and phone numbers to customer accounts.
- ERPNext is the system of record for customers, items, prices, stock, orders and invoices. The desk only reads it.
